Who We Are

Padu Security And Services Sdn. Bhd. is a Bumiputera-owned company based in Meru, Ipoh, incorporated on 3 May 2023. Licensed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (KDN) and registered with the Ministry of Finance, the company provides security services across Malaysia.

With a strong focus on understanding client needs, Padu offers tailored, high-quality security solutions to protect people, assets, and information. The company is committed to professionalism, customer service, and operational excellence, driven by well-trained and ethical security personnel.

  • Our Mandate: To provide secure and ethical protection solutions.
  • Our Purpose: To safeguard people, assets, and data through excellence.
